Application Stage Rules: Prototype

Applicant Tracking System · throwaway prototype for exploring design decisions

Nothing here is approved yet. Options marked Suggested are only starting points to compare against. Change any rule at any time and the whole page updates instantly.

The question: Which rules should control how staff move an application between hiring stages (Applied → Screening → Interview → Final Review → Offer → Hired, plus Rejected and Withdrawn)? Should staff be able to skip stages, move backward, or reopen an application that's already Hired, Rejected or Withdrawn? And when a job opening closes, should its applications be left unchanged or frozen until the job reopens?

How to use: Pick rules in the panel below. Then either click the stage buttons on any application (free play) or open a guided scenario on the right and press its steps in order. Green-outlined stage buttons are moves the current rules allow. Dashed buttons are blocked. You can still click a blocked button to see the reason.
